Affiliate disclosure & privacy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Short version: Some links on this site and on our social accounts are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a commission. You don't pay anything extra.

Amazon Associates

Useful Finds is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Other retailers and affiliate programs

We may also earn commissions from links to other retailers and through affiliate networks. These commissions come at no extra cost to you. The price you pay is the same whether or not you use our link.

How this affects what we publish

  • Links that can earn us money are marked "Paid link" on the site, and posts with affiliate links are labeled on social media.
  • We describe products based on manufacturer information and other public details. We don't claim to have personally tested a product unless we clearly say so for that product.
  • We don't publish made-up reviews, ratings, or testimonials.
  • We don't list prices on the site because they change often. The retailer's page shows the current price and availability, and that's the one that counts.
  • No brand pays us to be included in a guide. If that ever changes, the sponsored item will be clearly labeled.

Privacy

  • No tracking cookies of our own. This site doesn't use analytics, ad trackers, or tracking pixels, and it doesn't set cookies.
  • Retailers may set cookies. When you click a link to a retailer (such as Amazon), that retailer may set cookies so it can credit the sale to us. Their own privacy policies apply once you're on their site.
  • Fonts and hosting. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the site is served by a static hosting provider. Like any web server, these services may receive your IP address and basic browser information when a page loads.
  • Email. If you email us, we use your address only to reply.
